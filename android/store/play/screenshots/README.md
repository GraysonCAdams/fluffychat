# Phone screenshots

Play's phone screenshots go in this folder as `01.png`, `02.png` and so on, in listing order.

They are not captured from a phone. They come from the HTML recreations of Seam's screens in the
seam-site repository (`/home/gray/repos/seam-site` on GRAYTHINKPAD), rendered there so every
picture shows invented people and chats and no real conversation, account or contact.

What Play takes: JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha, 1080x1920 or larger at 9:16, at least 2 and
at most 8 (4 or more for promotional placements). Nothing on them may show another company's
logo, an iMessage or Google Messages look-alike, or a named server or bridge as a
recommendation. A network may appear only the way it would in a user's own chat list, as one of
several.

When the files land here, add one line per screenshot to `../../copy/play-alt-text.txt`, after
the feature graphic's line and in the same order; `node android/store/check.js` fails until the
counts match.
